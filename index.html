📝 Design Note (Factory Operations)

                
            

            

                
1. Assumptions Made

                
    
                    
  • Factory floor Wi-Fi can drop intermittently; data is saved instantly on local device storage so no dispatch slips are lost.
    • 
                    
  • Operators log into their respective plant station securely with credentials (e.g. `plant1` / `Plant1`).
    • 
                    
  • Two-way agreement: Transfers are only marked complete when the receiving plant taps "GOT IT".
    • 
                

                
2. Key Decisions & Trade-Offs

                
    
                    
  • Used client-side storage for instant deployment without heavy server setup.
    • 
                    
  • Stock limits prevent sending more parts than are actually in the plant ledger.
    • 
                

                
3. Future SAP Scalability & Next Steps

                
Although running standalone today, dispatches package clean standard JSON payloads designed to plug directly into SAP S/4HANA (MM/PP modules) via IDoc (DELVRY03).
